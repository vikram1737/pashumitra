About PashuMitra

An AI-powered platform for cattle breed identification and livestock health advisory, built for Indian farmers and veterinary practitioners.

🌿 Project Overview

PashuMitra combines computer vision and livestock health knowledge to help identify 40+ indigenous Indian cattle and buffalo breeds and assist with 12+ common livestock diseases.

Built to give farmers, students, and field workers fast access to breed profiles, symptom checklists, and care guidance.

🧠 Breed Classification Models

Four deep learning architectures were trained and compared (78.4% top accuracy):

  • MLP Baseline — flatten + dense layers
  • CNN from Scratch — 5 convolutional blocks + GAP
  • ResNet-50 Transfer Learning — ImageNet pretrained
  • ViT-B/16 Transfer Learning — Vision Transformer

📊 Datasets & Health Coverage

Breed Catalog: 40 indigenous Indian breeds (28 cattle + 12 buffalo breeds) with regional origin, yield, lifespan, and traits.

Disease Profiles (12+): FMD, Lumpy Skin Disease, Clinical Mastitis, Tick Fever (Babesiosis & Theileriosis), HS, Black Quarter, BRD, Brucellosis, Milk Fever, Bloat, and Anthrax.

🩺 Health Advisor Feature

The disease advisory module includes:

  • Symptom-based checklist matching 12+ cattle & buffalo diseases
  • Full disease profiles with causes, treatment, and prevention
  • Visual AI detection from uploaded animal photos
  • Vaccination and biosecurity reminders

⚙️ Tech Stack

  • PyTorch 2.x + torchvision + timm
  • FastAPI backend with Pydantic schemas
  • Responsive HTML5, CSS3 & JavaScript frontend
  • Rule-based + ML disease advisory engine

⚠️ Disclaimer

PashuMitra is an educational and informational tool; breed and health outputs do not replace veterinary examination.

Always consult a qualified veterinarian for medical diagnosis, prescriptions, and emergency treatment decisions.
